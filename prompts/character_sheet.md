LAYOUT
A single landscape sheet on a neutral mid-grey seamless background, evenly lit with no
dramatic shadows so the design can be matched later. Arrange, left to right:
1. full-body front view, arms relaxed;
2. three-quarter view;
3. profile view;
4. a head-and-shoulders close-up large enough to read facial structure;
5. a small palette strip of six swatches sampled from the wardrobe and the environment.

CONTINUITY REQUIREMENTS
- Identical face, hair, wardrobe, silhouette, age and body proportions in all views.
- Keep the same colour grading and lighting temperature across the sheet.
- Wardrobe must include every persistent detail (fabric texture, stitching, wear, accessories).
- No additional characters, no props not named above, no scene background: this sheet is
  the identity anchor that every keyframe in the film will be conditioned on.
