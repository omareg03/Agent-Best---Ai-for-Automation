# Ninth Legion package production notes

Evidence note, checked 2026-10-26: RIB 665 dates the York building inscription to AD 107–108 and says the gate was built through Legion IX Hispana. RIB's tile-stamp catalogue places the Ninth's base at York from AD 71 until its probable departure toward the end of Trajan's reign, while noting stamped tiles at multiple sites. A 2025 Britannia/Cambridge article discusses possible Ninth detachments at Nijmegen in the early second century and last attested officers around AD 124; its broader Hadrianic-crisis connection is an interpretation, not proof of a specific defeat. The exact fate, battle, final headcount, and a 5,000-soldier disappearance are not established by these sources. Do not state that the entire legion was annihilated in Scotland.

No paid model, Manim renderer, or vidIQ account was accessed. Verify dateable claims against linked records before upload; label every reconstruction; do not claim a full-strength legion was annihilated in Scotland.
